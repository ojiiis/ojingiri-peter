Ojingiri Peter’s practice explores the intersections of memory, cultural identity, and the fluid nature of the human form. Drawing from both classical portraiture and contemporary abstraction, his works serve as a visual dialogue between the past and an imagined future.

His paintings are characterized by a deep engagement with texture and light, often incorporating layered pigments to create a sense of ethereal depth. This process mirrors his interest in the "texture of time"—how history leaves its mark on both the physical world and the collective psyche.

Ojingiri (b. 1992, Lagos) has been the subject of several solo exhibitions and is increasingly recognized for his unique ability to translate complex emotional landscapes into vibrant, tactile experiences on canvas.
